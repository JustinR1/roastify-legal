Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2025

1. Information We Collect

RoastifyAI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Photos: When you use our camera roll roasting feature, you grant temporary access to selected photos from your device. These photos are analyzed using AI and are never stored on our servers. They are transmitted securely, processed, and immediately discarded.
  • Payment Information: Payment processing is handled securely through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We do not have access to or store your credit card information.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Identifier: We generate a secure, anonymous device identifier that persists across app reinstalls using your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain). This identifier is used solely to link your roasts, credit balance, and app data to your device, ensuring you maintain access to your purchase history and roast collection even if you reinstall the app.
  • Usage Data: We may collect information about how you interact with the app, including features used, time spent, and error logs to improve app performance.
  • Device Information: We may collect device type, operating system version for analytics and troubleshooting purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Generate personalized comedy roasts based on your photos
  • Process payments through Apple's In-App Purchase system
  • Improve our AI algorithms and app functionality
  • Provide customer support
  • Send important app updates and service notifications

3. Data Storage and Security

Your Privacy is Our Priority:

  • Photos: Never stored on our servers. Analyzed once and immediately discarded.
  • Roast History: We store generated roasts in our secure database (Supabase) including:
    • The roast text and meme quote generated by AI
    • Your selected comedy style (e.g., Savage, Wholesome)
    • Roast type (Quick Roast, Deep Dive)
    • Anonymous device identifier to link your roasts
    • Timestamp of when the roast was created
    We do NOT store your photos or any personally identifiable information beyond an anonymous device ID.
  • Credits and Purchases: Your credit balance and purchase history may be stored to track your account status.
  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • OpenAI: For AI-powered photo analysis and roast generation. Data is processed according to OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • Supabase: Secure cloud database for storing roast history and user data. Data is stored in secure, encrypted databases.
  • Apple In-App Purchases: For secure payment processing. Apple's privacy policy governs payment data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We share data with trusted service providers (OpenAI, Apple) solely to provide app functionality.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: You can request access to your data (roast history, credit balance, device ID) by contacting us directly.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your roast history and account data by contacting us at privacy@rollcall.app. This will permanently delete all roasts associated with your device ID.
  • Push Notifications: You can opt out of promotional communications by disabling push notifications in the app settings.
